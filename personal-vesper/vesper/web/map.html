Touch anywhere to wake

How it works

Moving · speaking · keeping

Moving. Drag to turn the whole structure. Pinch or scroll to fly in and out. Tap any point to open it; tap the core to talk. Centre puts you back if you get lost.

Speaking. Say “Vesper” and then what you want. It answers out loud and flies to whatever you name.

Talking into a branch. Press and hold any point — however small or deep — and it starts listening into that point. Everything you then say becomes something underneath it, one after another, no need to say “add” or name the branch. Questions and commands still work while you're in there. It lets go after twenty seconds of quiet.

It builds as well as reads. Try:

  • new project called Northwind
  • add buy the domain to Trendkept
  • mark buy the domain done
  • go to paper log · status

Chrome listens as soon as this opens. Safari needs one touch per visit before any page may use the microphone — Apple's rule, not a setting — so your first touch anywhere switches it on. The screen is held awake while it listens.

Keeping it. The map lives in this browser only. Clear your history or change device and it is gone. Export writes a file; Import reads it back. Do that before you put anything into it you would mind losing.
